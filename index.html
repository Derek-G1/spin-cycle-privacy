Spin Cycle Privacy Policy

RadStorm Tech · Effective 27 September 2026

Spin Cycle is a game for Android made by RadStorm Tech ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: we don't collect any personal information, and the game has no ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your progress, high scores, coins, unlocked items and settings are saved in the app's own storage on your phone. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them.

Google Play Games

If you sign in to Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores to Google so they appear on your Play Games profile. Google handles your sign-in and profile under the Google Privacy Policy. We don't receive your email address or contacts. You can play without signing in.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google of which item you bought, so it can unlock it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts with us, and no personal information collected by us.
  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share any information, because we don't have any.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
